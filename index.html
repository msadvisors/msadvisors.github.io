Products in Kronox’s portfolio

Kronox Lab Sciences, built from 2008 by Mr Ketan Ramani, Mr Pritesh Ramani and Mr Jogindersingh Jaswal into a specialty fine chemicals manufacturer serving pharmaceutical, nutraceutical and biotech customers, was listed on NSE and BSE in 2024. Its promoters have divested a 64.26% controlling stake to Indo Borax & Chemicals Limited, triggering a mandatory open offer to public shareholders under the SEBI (SAST) Regulations. MS Advisors led the mandate through a six-month process, working alongside CNK & Associates LLP, Sterling Associates as legal counsel, and the acquirer’s advisers, Sidwim, Deloitte and Cyril Amarchand Mangaldas. The promoters retain a 9.95% stake and will support a structured transition, passing the business to a well-capitalised listed group with complementary operations positioned to fund its next phase of growth.

“When founders of that calibre transition control, price is only one variable. Stewardship of the institution is the other, and it is the harder one to underwrite.”

Mitanshu Shah, Founder & CEO

Financial Structuring / M&A

We advise promoters and boards through the capital-raising and ownership decisions that define a company’s next stage, from listing to acquisition to expansion financing. Each mandate is led end to end, from structuring through negotiation to close.
