Change of control — listed to listed

Kronox Lab Sciences / Indo Borax & Chemicals

MS Advisors, with CNK & Associates LLP, acted as exclusive Strategic and M&A Advisors to the promoters of Kronox Lab Sciences Limited.

NSE: KRONOX · BSE: 544187  →  NSE: INDOBORAX · BSE: 524342

64.26%Controlling stake divested
9.95%Promoter stake retained
20 Aug 2026Agreement signed
~185Products in Kronox’s portfolio

Kronox Lab Sciences was built from 2008 by Mr Ketan Ramani, Mr Pritesh Ramani and Mr Jogindersingh Jaswal into a specialty fine chemicals manufacturer serving pharmaceutical, nutraceutical and biotech customers, and listed on NSE and BSE in 2024. Its promoters have divested a 64.26% controlling stake to Indo Borax & Chemicals Limited, triggering a mandatory open offer to public shareholders under the SEBI (SAST) Regulations.

MS Advisors led the mandate through a six-month process alongside CNK & Associates LLP, Sterling Associates as legal counsel, and the acquirer’s advisers — Sidwim, Deloitte and Cyril Amarchand Mangaldas. The promoters retain a 9.95% stake and will support a structured transition, passing the business to a well-capitalised listed group with complementary operations positioned to fund its next phase of growth.

“When founders of that calibre transition control, price is only one variable. Stewardship of the institution is the other, and it is the harder one to underwrite.”
Mitanshu Shah · Founder & CEO
